I am a recent graduate seeking opportunities in Environmental Politics. Holding dual Master’s degrees in International Politics (KU Leuven) and Environmental Sciences and Management (ULB), I have developed a thorough understanding of global environmental challenges, the skills to design and monitor management strategies, and a solid command of European and international political dynamics<sup>1</sup>. This interdisciplinary foundation allows me to effectively translate scientific evidence into actionable political strategies that are both ambitious and pragmatically feasible.

With solid writing and analytical skills, I specialize in distilling intricate environmental and regulatory data into clear, concise, and actionable insights tailored for diverse audiences. Both of my Master’s theses were awarded magna cum laude, showcasing my methodological discipline, analytical precision, and ability to manage large datasets.

Fluent in French (native), English (C1), and Dutch (C1), with an advanced level of Spanish (B2), I bring strong intercultural and diplomatic communication skills shaped by diverse experiences across Africa and Latin America. My profile is further complemented by proficiency in the Office suite, Canva, LaTeX, NVivo, and AI technologies (including Generative AI and notions of Machine and Deep Learning).

I am an organized, adaptable professional who thrives on structure and prioritization. While I am a resourceful, autonomous learner, I am also a people-oriented team player who places great importance on building trust and fostering a respectful, dynamic work atmosphere. These qualities enable me to perform in fast-paced settings with responsibility, meticulousness, and engagement.
