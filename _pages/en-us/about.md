With dual master’s degrees in <b>International Politics</b> (KUL) and <b>Environmental Sciences & Management</b> (ULB), I work at the intersection of sustainability and public policy. My interdisciplinary background allows me to navigate the chain of environmental governance and the various stages of the policy lifecycle, from raw environmental data to the final diplomatic brief. In this role, I specialize in translating environmental and regulatory data into clear, accessible insights tailored to diverse audiences and decision-making contexts.

Working languages: French (C2) \| English (C1) \| Dutch (C1) \| Spanish (B2)
